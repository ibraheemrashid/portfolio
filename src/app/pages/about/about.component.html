About

How I work

A few things that don't show up on a résumé.

  • I'd rather understand the actual problem than jump straight to code — left over from sales, where nobody cares about your solution if you didn't listen first.
  • I explain things the way I used to explain code to teenagers at Codeyoung: plainly, no jargon, until it actually lands.
  • Small mistakes compound fast — I've seen it lose a sale, and I've seen it break a build. I pay attention to detail because of that.
  • I'd rather ship something real and slightly rough than spend months polishing something nobody's using yet.
